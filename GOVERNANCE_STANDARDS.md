# Operational Governance & Integrity Standards

## 1. Compliance First
All documentation, workflows, and assets delivered by the Operator must strictly adhere to global corporate governance standards. Ambiguity is the primary source of organizational entropy and is systematically eliminated through structured mapping.

## 2. Documentation Standards (SSOT)
* **Single Source of Truth (SSOT):** Information redundancy is strictly prohibited. Each process must reside in a single, authorized location.
* **Linguistic Precision:** Terminological accuracy is non-negotiable. Translations and technical content must reflect the exactitude required in high-stakes environments.
* **Traceability:** Every protocol iteration is recorded to ensure full auditability and system integrity.

## 3. Operator Standards
* The Operator acts as a Systems Architect.
* Mediocrity is not accepted; it is identified and removed.
* Excellence is not a target; it is the baseline operational state.

## 4. Audit & Review
These protocols are subject to continuous review. Compliance is monitored to ensure system execution remains perfectly aligned with long-term strategic objectives.
